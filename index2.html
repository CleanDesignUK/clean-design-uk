Frequently asked

Questions before we get started?

Here are the answers to a few things clients commonly ask. If yours is not here, send us a message and we will be happy to help.

Ask us a question
How much does a business website cost?
Our website packages are tailored to the size, features and goals of each project. After a free conversation, we provide a clear quote with no hidden fees, so you know exactly what is included before work begins.
Do you handle hosting and maintenance?
Yes. We can provide managed hosting with SSL, backups and performance monitoring, plus ongoing care for updates, security and website improvements.
Can you improve my existing website?
Absolutely. We can audit your current website, improve speed and usability, refresh the design or rebuild it where that would produce a stronger result.
Do you provide copywriting and SEO?
Yes. We can help shape conversion-focused page copy, keyword structure and on-page SEO so your website launches with strong foundations.
How long does a typical project take?
A brochure website commonly takes around three to four weeks, while larger or e-commerce builds may take six to eight weeks. Your exact timeline is agreed before the project starts.
