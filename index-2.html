Ask about your project ↗
What does Clean Design UK do?+

We design and redesign business websites and ecommerce stores, create business branding, deliver digital marketing and SEO support, and build GoHighLevel funnels and automations. Every project is shaped around a commercial goal such as more enquiries, bookings or sales.

Do you only work with businesses in North Wales?+

No. We are rooted in North Wales and regularly serve businesses across Anglesey, Bangor, Caernarfon, Conwy, Llandudno and Wrexham. Our remote process also works for clients throughout Wales and the wider UK.

How much does a business website cost?+

The cost depends on what the website must do, the number of page layouts, content requirements and any ecommerce or automation features. After a free consultation, you receive a clear written proposal before work begins.

Can you redesign my website without losing its SEO?+

Yes. We audit the current content, URLs, rankings and technical setup. Valuable pages are retained or improved, URL changes are mapped carefully and the redesigned site is checked before launch.

Is SEO included with website design?+

Every website is built with strong on-page and technical foundations, including clear structure, metadata, mobile performance, crawlable content and appropriate structured data. Ongoing SEO can be added when needed.

Can you help my business appear in Google and AI search?+

Yes. We make your services, expertise, locations and business details easy for search engines and answer engines to understand through useful content, internal linking, entity signals and structured data.

How long does a website project take?+

A focused brochure website usually takes around three to four weeks. Larger or more complex builds often take six to eight weeks, depending on content, features and feedback.

What GoHighLevel support do you provide?+

We can help with landing pages, forms, calendars, pipelines, workflows, email and SMS sequences, payments, membership journeys and integrations.
