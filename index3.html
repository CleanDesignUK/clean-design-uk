Straight answers

Web design questions small businesses ask us.

Useful answers before you decide whether Clean Design UK is the right fit for your project.

How much does a small business website cost?

Our entry-level website packages start from £250. The final cost depends on the number of pages, functionality, content requirements and whether you need extras such as e-commerce, booking systems, branding or ongoing marketing.

Do you only work with businesses in North Wales?

No. We are based in North Wales and work with many businesses across Anglesey, Bangor, Gwynedd and the wider region, but we also build websites for clients throughout Wales and the rest of the UK.

Will my website be built with SEO in mind?

Yes. Our builds include search-friendly page structure, mobile responsiveness, performance considerations, metadata, internal linking and on-page SEO foundations. Location-focused businesses can also be structured around the areas and services they want to target.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We can redesign an existing site to improve its appearance, mobile usability, navigation, conversion journey, speed and search foundations without starting the business itself from scratch.
